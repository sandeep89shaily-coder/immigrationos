# RCIC visit — real workflow validation guide (synthetic data only)

**Purpose:** Validate what an actual practising RCIC needs from a competing CRM,
not simply what vendors advertise. Use this in the upcoming RCIC visit.

**Privacy requirement:** Ask them to demonstrate with **fictional records or
redacted samples**. Do not photograph, copy, upload, screen-record, or enter
real client names, passport numbers, bank details, medical/immigration records,
or message contents into ImmigrationOS or ChatGPT.

## Start with a representative 15–30 minute walkthrough

Ask the RCIC to demonstrate (using a dummy or fictional client):

1. How a web/WhatsApp/phone lead becomes a booked consultation and where a
   signed *consultation agreement* appears.
2. How they handle conflict/previous-representative checks and create, amend,
   e-sign, and send the *service agreement*.
3. What exact records they reuse when they create the application, including
   spouse, dependents, immigration, address, work, travel and education history.
4. How documents are requested, collected, corrected, versioned, expired and
   matched to the right client/case.
5. How they produce **IMM 5476**, one other common form, and a barcode form:
   original form version, fields not autofilled, signature, Adobe validation.
6. How they create and review the submission package, track IRCC submission,
   upload AOR/ADR, and notify the client **in writing**.
7. How they assign a critical response deadline, to whom it escalates, and
   what happens when a reminder fails.
8. How fees, taxes, disbursements, installments and **client trust advances**
   are distinguished; which system holds the actual trust ledger.
9. What messages (email, calls, WhatsApp, portal) get into the client file
   automatically, and how internal-only notes are prevented from leaking.
10. How the matter closes: final invoice, trust refund, return of originals,
    client notification, file export and retention controls.
11. How staff roles work: receptionist, assistant, filing professional, accounts,
    branch manager and external auditor; how a departing employee loses access.
12. What happens if the RCIC is suddenly unavailable: backup professional,
    continuity plan, handoff and client access.

## Three quantitative questions

- **Time:** For your most common case type, how much *person-time* does your
  office spend from signed retainer to reviewed first draft, excluding IRCC
  processing waits?
- **Rework:** Which three tasks are repeated most, and how often must staff
  re-enter the same facts or ask for the same document?
- **Financial and compliance risk:** What has actually caused a missed
  deadline, trust-reconciliation error, client complaint, or long follow-up
  exchange (without discussing identifiable client events)?

## Product scoring sheet

Rate each from 0 (painful/unavailable) to 5 (works reliably):
- Prospect and consultation flow __/5
- Retainers, signatures and conflict checks __/5
- Client data reuse across a family __/5
- Form autofill and **official** validation __/5
- Document requests and feedback __/5
- Deadline management __/5
- Client communications __/5
- Trust/billing visibility __/5
- Supervision and role permissions __/5
- Closeout/export/continuity __/5

Record for each: vendor/tool used, screenshots of **synthetic UI only** (if
permitted), time taken, failure mode, workaround and importance to the RCIC.

## Final interview prompt

> If ImmigrationOS could remove only **three daily frustrations** while keeping
> you fully in control of advice, signatures, client funds and submissions,
> which three would make you switch from your current software?

## Do not assume

- A vendor's advertised feature works reliably on the RCIC's selected plan.
- A generated PDF with text is a correctly validated IRCC form.
- A Canadian-hosting claim proves PIPEDA/CICC compliance.
- Payment receipts prove correct trust handling.
- Every firm uses identical workflows.
- The RCIC can authorize disclosure of client data for a development demo.

## What to bring back for development

- The 3 most common real application **types** (not applicant details).
- Form codes/revisions that consume the most staff time.
- Five daily repetitive actions and approximate minutes lost.
- The firm's current software **name and plan**, if they agree to share.
- Exact workflow bottlenecks and what a credible demo must achieve.
